Our Story

CyberCloak was born from a simple idea: what if everyone could have access to military-grade encryption without needing to be a cybersecurity expert?

Founded by Sagar S and his team of talented BTech students from Bapuji Institute of Technology, we've been working tirelessly to democratize digital privacy since 2025.

Our mission is to make advanced steganography and encryption technologies accessible to everyone, from individuals protecting personal photos to businesses securing sensitive data.

Our Technology

Built with the latest security standards

Encryption Standards

  • AES-256: Military-grade symmetric encryption
  • PBKDF2: Password-based key derivation
  • SHA-256: Secure hash algorithm
  • RSA-4096: Asymmetric encryption for key exchange

Steganography Methods

  • LSB: Least Significant Bit manipulation
  • DCT: Discrete Cosine Transform
  • Wavelet: Wavelet-based hiding
  • Adaptive: Dynamic capacity optimization
