Quick answers to common questions

The process is designed to be simple and secure!

We support the following image formats:
  • PNG - Recommended for best results
  • JPEG - Good for photos
  • GIF - Supports animation
  • BMP - Uncompressed format

PNG is recommended as it preserves image quality without compression artifacts.

CyberCloak uses military-grade security:
  • AES-256 encryption - The same standard used by governments
  • PBKDF2 key derivation - Protects against brute force attacks
  • Zero-logging policy - We don't store your messages or passwords
  • Client-side processing - Your data never leaves your device

Message capacity depends on your image:
  • Image size - Larger images can hide more data
  • Image complexity - Detailed images work better
  • Format - PNG typically offers the best capacity

Our system automatically calculates the maximum capacity when you upload an image. Typical images can hide several KB of text data.

Step-by-Step Tutorials

Learn how to use CyberCloak like a pro

🔐

Encrypting Your First Message

  1. Sign in to your CyberCloak account
  2. Click "New Encryption" on your dashboard
  3. Upload a high-quality image (PNG recommended)
  4. Enter your secret message
  5. Add a strong password (optional but recommended)
  6. Click "Process Message" and wait for completion
  7. Download your encrypted image
🔓

Extracting Hidden Messages

  1. Go to the homepage or use the extraction tool
  2. Upload the encrypted image
  3. Enter the password if one was used
  4. Click "Extract Message"
  5. View your revealed message
  6. Copy or save the message as needed
